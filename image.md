# Images to shoot or generate

Sixteen files. Every one of them has a slot already wired into the site — drop
the file in at the exact path below and the page picks it up with no code
change. Until then `SmartImage` renders a branded pine-green panel, so nothing
is broken while you work through this list.

Each image has **one paragraph**, written to be pasted straight into an image
model as-is. The house style is baked into every paragraph rather than assumed,
so you can generate them one at a time, out of order, weeks apart, and they
will still look like one set.

---

## House style (already inside every prompt below — here for reference)

Full-frame camera, 35mm or 50mm prime, natural light only. Warm, slightly
desaturated grade: deep green and terracotta in the palette, creamy off-white
highlights, film-like grain, gentle contrast. Editorial travel photography, not
tour brochure — no HDR, no oversaturated skies, no lens flare, no text or logos
in frame, no watermarks. People appear mid-activity and unposed, never looking
at the camera, never centred and smiling.

**Season matters.** Jawai and Udaipur frames are **early September, the tail of
the monsoon**: green grass on the granite, a full dam, heavy broken cloud, wet
stone. Jodhpur frames are **late October, post-monsoon**: dry clear light, warm
dust, hard blue shadows. Don't mix them.

**Export**: JPEG, quality ~82, sRGB, under about 1.5MB each. Keep the
unprocessed original in `raw-images/` — it's gitignored — following the
existing `<set>-<slot>-<subject>.png` naming.

# 1. Jawai — `public/images/jawai/`

This is the hero set for Escape 001. Jawai is the emotional centre of the trip,
so these carry the most weight of anything in this document.

### `hero.jpg` — 16:9, ≥2400px
A wide editorial landscape photograph of the Jawai hills in Rajasthan at first
light in early September, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm prime in
natural light. Enormous smooth granite domes rise straight out of flat scrub
country, their rounded backs catching low warm sun while the shadowed sides
stay a deep cool grey-green. Monsoon has just ended, so thin green grass covers
the ground between the boulders and the sky carries heavy broken cloud with
gaps of pale gold rather than a flat blue. A single dirt track curves through
the lower third of the frame toward the rocks, giving scale. Warm and slightly
desaturated grade with deep green and terracotta in the palette, creamy
off-white highlights, fine film grain, gentle contrast. No people, no vehicles,
no text or logos, no HDR, no oversaturated sky, no lens flare.

### `gallery-1.jpg` — 4:3, ≥1600px

shepherd is in the middle distance in a white turban and dhoti, seen from
behind and slightly to the side, mid-stride and entirely unaware of the camera;
the goats spread loosely around him and the dust they raise catches the low
afternoon sun. Green post-monsoon grass underfoot, a smooth granite dome
filling the background under broken cloud. Warm slightly desaturated grade,
deep green and terracotta palette, creamy off-white highlights, fine film

### `gallery-3.jpg` — 4:5, ≥1400px

### `gallery-5.jpg` — 1:1, ≥1200px
A square editorial photograph of a small bonfire on open ground below the

### `gallery-6.jpg` — 1:1, ≥1200px
A square editorial landscape photograph of the Jawai Bandh reservoir in
Rajasthan at early morning in September, shot on a full-frame camera with a
50mm prime in natural light. Still, wide water reaching to a low horizon of
rounded granite hills, thin mist sitting on the surface, a scatter of wading
birds in the far middle distance small enough to read as texture rather than
subject. The dam is full after the monsoon; the near shore in the bottom of the
frame is wet dark rock and green grass. Cool blue-green water against warm
morning light on the far hills. Warm slightly desaturated grade, creamy
off-white highlights, fine film grain, gentle contrast. No people, no boats, no
text or logos, no HDR.

# 3. The Outway set — `public/images/outway/`

Not a place. These four carry the brand argument — that you come for the
destination and leave remembering the people — and they are used on the
homepage, the About page and the brochure's community spread rather than on any
one trip. Shoot these on a real departure; they are the hardest to fake and the
most valuable when they're real.

### `the-table.jpg` — 16:9, ≥2000px
A wide, warm, unposed editorial photograph of ten or eleven travellers of mixed
ages and backgrounds eating dinner together at one long shared table outdoors
in Rajasthan on an early-September evening, shot on a full-frame camera with a
35mm prime by string light and candlelight only. Shot from the end of the table
at seated height so the perspective runs down its length: shared dishes in the
middle, hands reaching and passing, two people mid-laugh turned toward each
other, one person listening. Nobody looks at the camera and nobody is centred.
Warm slightly desaturated grade, deep green and terracotta palette, creamy
off-white highlights, fine film grain, gentle contrast, soft falloff into
darkness at the edges. No text or logos, no HDR, no flash.

### `story-circle.jpg` — 4:5, ≥1400px
A vertical, intimate, unposed editorial photograph of a small group sitting
close around a low bonfire under a heavy field of stars in rural Rajasthan on a
clear early-September night, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm prime
using firelight and starlight only. One person is speaking, hands mid-gesture
and lit warm from below; the others are half-lit and turned toward them,
listening. Faces are partly in shadow and nobody acknowledges the camera. The
vertical frame gives the top third to the star field above the dark silhouette
of granite hills. Warm slightly desaturated grade, terracotta and deep blue
palette, creamy off-white highlights, fine film grain, gentle contrast. No text
or logos, no HDR, no flash, no light painting.

### `the-letter.jpg` — 1:1, ≥1200px
A square editorial detail photograph of a person writing a short handwritten
note on a folded piece of cream paper, resting on their knee, on the last
evening of a trip in Udaipur in early September, shot on a full-frame camera
with a 50mm prime in soft natural evening light. Close and shallow: the hand,
the pen and the top edge of the paper are sharp, the handwriting deliberately
illegible, and the background dissolves into out-of-focus warm lamplight and
lake-reflected dusk. No face in frame. Warm slightly desaturated grade, deep
green and terracotta palette, creamy off-white highlights, fine film grain,
gentle contrast. No readable text of any kind, no logos, no HDR.

### `the-road.jpg` — 16:9, ≥2000px
A wide editorial photograph taken from inside a moving vehicle on the road
between Jawai and Udaipur in Rajasthan on an early-September afternoon, shot on
a full-frame camera with a 35mm prime in natural light. Framed past the
shoulder and elbow of a passenger resting an arm on the open window in the
soft-focus foreground, through to a sunlit two-lane road unwinding into green
post-monsoon Aravalli hills under heavy broken cloud. Slight motion blur in the
verge, warm dusty light across the dashboard edge. No faces, no eye contact.
Warm slightly desaturated grade, deep green and terracotta palette, creamy
off-white highlights, fine film grain, gentle contrast. No text or logos, no
HDR, no oversaturated sky, no lens flare.

## After you drop the files in

Nothing to deploy for the Jawai and Jodhpur sets that are referenced from the
database — but the trip rows point at these paths, so the filenames have to
match exactly. If you'd rather not touch the repo at all, the uploader in
**Admin → Trips → edit → Content** writes to Supabase Storage and updates the
trip row directly, which changes the photo without a redeploy.

The `outway/` set is referenced from code, so those four need a push.

Existing folders, for context: `public/images/udaipur/` is the Udaipur shoot,
and `public/images/escape-001/` is the older Mount Abu shoot — that folder name
predates the renumbering and now belongs to Escape 003, so don't read anything
into it.
