# Escape 001 — image prompt pack

Paragraph prompts for generating the visuals the site expects. Everything is
written for **mid-August monsoon in Rajasthan**: green Aravallis, heavy skies,
wet stone, full lakes. That season is the whole pitch of this trip, so please
don't substitute dry golden-hour desert imagery — it would misrepresent what
travellers actually get.

## House style — apply to every prompt

Shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm or 50mm prime, natural light only, no
flash. Warm, slightly desaturated colour grade with deep green and terracotta
in the palette and creamy off-white highlights, film-like grain, gentle
contrast. Editorial travel photography, the register of a good magazine feature
rather than a tour brochure — no HDR, no oversaturated skies, no lens flares,
no text or watermarks or logos anywhere in the frame. People appear
mid-activity and unposed, never looking at the camera and never arranged in a
line. Where faces appear they should read as Indian travellers in their
twenties and thirties in ordinary contemporary clothing.

## 1. `escape-001/hero.jpg` — the anchor image

A wide cinematic view across Lake Pichola in Udaipur at the very end of golden
hour during monsoon, photographed from a low boat-level vantage point so the
water fills the bottom third of the frame. The white marble facade of the City
Palace complex rises along the far shore, its windows just beginning to glow
warm amber against a sky of heavy blue-grey monsoon cloud broken by a band of
apricot light near the horizon. The lake is full and calm with soft
reflections. A small traditional wooden boat with a handful of passengers sits
in the middle distance as a sense of scale, silhouetted rather than detailed.
The hills behind the palace are unusually green from the rains. Shot on a
full-frame camera at 35mm, natural light, warm desaturated film grade, gentle
grain, generous empty sky in the upper left third for a headline to sit over.
No text, no logos, no people in the foreground.

## 2. `escape-001/gallery-1.jpg` — dinner at the ghat

An evening scene on an open rooftop restaurant terrace in Udaipur, shot from
behind and slightly to the side of a small group of young Indian travellers
seated around a low table with thali plates and glasses, mid-conversation, none
of them looking at the camera. Beyond the terrace parapet the dark water of the
lake, and across it the floodlit City Palace glowing warm gold against a deep
indigo night sky with monsoon cloud. Warm practical lighting on the terrace —
hanging bulbs and candles — falling off quickly into the dark, so the palace
across the water is the brightest thing in the frame. Shallow depth of field,
50mm, natural and available light only, warm film grade with rich shadows. No
text, no logos.

## 3. `escape-001/gallery-2.jpg` — the monsoon Aravalli drive

A wide landscape of the Aravalli hills between Udaipur and Mount Abu in full
monsoon green, photographed from a bend on the ghat road. A narrow tarmac road
curves through the frame from lower right into the middle distance, wet and
dark from recent rain with reflections of the sky in it. The hills on either
side are dense green scrub forest, low cloud sitting in the valleys and
snagging on the ridgelines. The sky is layered grey and silver with a break of
pale light in the distance. Nothing man-made in the frame except the road.
Shot at 35mm on a full-frame camera, overcast natural light, cool-leaning but
warm-graded, soft contrast, film grain. No vehicles, no text, no logos.

## 4. `escape-001/gallery-3.jpg` — Dilwara marble (portrait)

A vertical interior detail of intricately carved white marble in a
900-year-old Jain temple in the Dilwara style — a ceiling dome and supporting
pillar covered edge to edge in extraordinarily fine floral and figurative
relief carving, the marble so thin in places that daylight glows softly
through it. Cool diffuse daylight entering from one side, no artificial light,
deep soft shadows in the recesses of the carving. Composed looking upward and
slightly off-centre so the dome fills the upper two thirds. Absolutely no
people in the frame. Shot at 35mm, natural light, high detail, restrained
contrast, warm-neutral grade with the marble reading as soft ivory rather than
clinical white. No text, no logos.

## 5. `escape-001/gallery-4.jpg` — Guru Shikhar sunrise (square)

A square-format sunrise from the highest ridge of the Aravalli range, looking
out over a sea of cloud filling the valleys below with only the dark tips of
farther ridges emerging. The sky above is clear pale gold shading to blue, the
sun just clearing the horizon and rimming the cloud edges. In the lower third
of the frame, the backs of two or three young travellers sitting on rock at
the edge, wrapped in light jackets, small in the composition and facing away
into the view. Shot at 35mm, natural backlight, cool blue shadows against warm
highlight, film grain, no lens flare, no text, no logos.

## 6. `escape-001/gallery-5.jpg` — Nakki Lake, late afternoon (square)

A square-format view of Nakki Lake in Mount Abu on an overcast monsoon
afternoon, water flat and pewter-coloured, surrounded by green wooded hills
with mist hanging low over the far shore. A few small painted rowing boats sit
tied along the near bank, empty, their colours the only saturation in an
otherwise muted frame. A single figure walks along the bank path in the middle
distance, small and unposed. Shot at 50mm, soft overcast light, low contrast,
muted warm-neutral grade, film grain. No text, no logos.

## Destination page — Udaipur

### 7. `udaipur/hero.jpg`

A wide elevated view over the old city of Udaipur at dusk during monsoon, taken
from a rooftop looking across a dense field of whitewashed and pale ochre
buildings toward the lake and the palace beyond. Lights are beginning to come
on across the city in small warm points. The sky is a deep gradient of slate
blue and warm grey with monsoon cloud, and the green hills are visible on the
far horizon. Shot at 35mm, blue-hour natural light, warm shadows, film grain,
generous sky. No text, no logos.

### 8–11. `udaipur/gallery-1.jpg` to `gallery-4.jpg` (all square, 1:1)

**gallery-1** — A close, quiet detail of a stepped stone ghat leading down into
lake water in Udaipur, the lowest steps submerged after monsoon rain, wet stone
dark and textured, a scattering of marigold petals on the surface. Soft
overcast light, no people, 50mm, muted warm grade, film grain.

**gallery-2** — A narrow lane in Udaipur's old city, whitewashed walls with
faded blue and ochre painted doorways, worn stone underfoot still wet from
rain. A scooter parked to one side and a figure walking away from camera at the
far end, small in the frame. Overcast diffuse light, 35mm, warm desaturated
grade, film grain. No text, no signage in a readable language.

**gallery-3** — A traditional wooden boat cutting slowly across the flat water
of Lake Pichola in the early morning, shot from the shore at a distance so the
boat is small against the water. Mist over the far end of the lake, the pale
outline of the island palace barely visible in it. Soft cool morning light,
very low contrast, 50mm, film grain. No text, no logos.

**gallery-4** — An overhead detail of a Rajasthani thali on a brass plate on a
dark wooden table — small steel bowls of dal, sabzi and curd, hot bajra roti,
a wedge of lime — lit by soft window light from one side. Half a hand entering
the frame at the edge, mid-serve. Shot from directly above at 50mm, natural
light, warm rich grade, shallow falloff at the corners, film grain. No text,
no logos, no branding on any vessel.

## Before you upload

- Export as JPEG, quality ~82, sRGB. Next.js re-encodes to AVIF/WebP on
  delivery so oversized source files just slow the build.
- Keep each file under about 1.5MB.
- The admin trip editor now has a real drag-and-drop uploader (Trips → edit →
  Content) that pushes to Supabase Storage — use that for anything you want to
  swap later without a deploy. The `public/images` paths above are the
  build-time defaults.
- Strip GPS EXIF from anything shot on a phone before publishing.
