# Supabase auth email templates

The six emails Supabase sends on our behalf — signup confirmation, password
reset, magic link, email change, invite, reauthentication. They are **not** sent
by `src/lib/email.ts`, so nothing in this repo controls them. They live in the
Supabase dashboard and have to be pasted in by hand.

Supabase dashboard → **Authentication → Emails → Templates**.

---

## Two settings, two different problems

People conflate these constantly, so, plainly:

| Problem | Where it is fixed |
|---|---|
| Mail arrives from `noreply@mail.app.supabase.io`, not from us | **SMTP Settings** — sender identity |
| Mail arrives from us but looks like a system alert with no logo | **Templates**, below — the message body |

Setting SMTP does not restyle the email. Pasting a template does not change the
sender. You need both.

### The SMTP half

Project Settings → **Authentication → SMTP Settings**:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Host | `smtp.resend.com` |
| Port | `465` |
| Username | `resend` |
| Password | your `RESEND_API_KEY` |
| Sender email | `noreply@outway.club` |
| Sender name | `Outway Club` |

The DNS side of this is already live and verified — SPF, DMARC, both DKIM keys
and the Zoho/Resend MX split are all in place (see
[`production-setup.md`](production-setup.md)). Nothing to add at Porkbun.

> **The built-in sender is not a production option.** Supabase rate-limits it to
> a handful of messages an hour and says so in their own docs. On a launch day
> where twenty people sign up, most of them silently never receive a
> confirmation link.

## About the logo

Three separate things get called "the logo", and only the second one is what
these templates control.

**1. The logo inside the email body.** What the templates below add. It has to
be an `<img>` pointing at a publicly reachable absolute URL — email clients
cannot see your project, your CSS, or anything on Zoho. These use
`https://outway.club/brand/logo-email.png`, which is generated by
`scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs` at 144px and 9.5KB.

Do **not** swap it for `/brand/logo.png`: that one is 167KB, and Gmail clips a
message at roughly 102KB, hiding everything past the cut behind "View entire
message" — including your button.

**2. The avatar beside the sender name in Gmail.** Not controlled by the email
at all. That circle comes from BIMI, which needs DMARC at `p=quarantine` or
`p=reject` (we are at `p=none` today) **plus** a Verified Mark Certificate — a
paid certificate that requires a registered trademark. It is a real thing worth
doing eventually; it is not a template setting, and it is not quick.

**3. The logo uploaded in Zoho Mail.** This is the one that will not do what you
want. Zoho's organisation logo brands *Zoho's own webmail interface* — what you
see when you log in to read mail. It is never attached to outbound messages, and
it has no effect whatsoever on mail that Supabase and Resend send, which never
touches Zoho. That upload is not broken; it is simply for something else.

## Before you paste

- Set **Site URL** to `https://outway.club` and add
  `https://outway.club/auth/callback` under **Redirect URLs**, or every link
  below resolves to `localhost:3000`.
- `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` is filled in by Supabase. Leave it exactly as
  written, braces and spaces included.
- Subject lines are set in the field above the body, not in the HTML.
- Send yourself one of each after pasting. The preview pane in the dashboard
  does not render like Gmail does.

## After pasting

- [ ] SMTP settings saved and sender shows `Outway Club <noreply@outway.club>`
- [ ] Site URL is `https://outway.club`, `/auth/callback` in Redirect URLs
- [ ] Signed up with a real Gmail address — mail arrives, logo renders, button works
- [ ] Requested a password reset — same
- [ ] Gmail → **⋮ → Show original** shows SPF, DKIM and DMARC all `PASS`
- [ ] Neither mail landed in spam
- [ ] The confirmation link lands on `outway.club`, not `localhost:3000`

If the logo shows as a broken image, open the URL in a browser. It is served
from `public/brand/`, so it only exists on a deploy that includes it — a link to
a preview deploy URL will break the moment that deploy is torn down, which is
why the templates hard-code the apex domain.
